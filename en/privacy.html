Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-22 08:03 PST

Effective October 2, 2026.

  • What we collect: your email address, preferred language and which editions you want to receive (Korea and/or U.S.) when you subscribe. You can change editions from the link at the end of any letter. To learn how readers find us, your browser keeps for 30 days the name of the outside site you first came from (e.g. youtube.com, without paths or search terms), the first page you saw, and any tag on our own links (?ref=youtube); these are recorded only if you subscribe, along with your optional answer to “How did you hear about us?”.   • Processors: Buttondown (list and email delivery), GitHub Pages (hosting), Cloudflare (visit totals, no cookies), Google Fonts, Google Apps Script and Sheets (unsubscribe requests and the optional reason you pick — no email address, only the link's identifier).

Use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at Google account permissions.
  • Deletion: unsubscribe from any email, or write to us and we will remove you.
