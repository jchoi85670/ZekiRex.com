How we score: everything we say gets scored

We score our calls, our stocks and our account in four layers, and publish hits and misses alike. The rules are set in advance; when they change, we say why.

#WhatHow
1Last issue checkYesterday's calls — and the condition that would prove them wrong — checked against the next close and marked hit / miss at the top of the letter.
2Featured stocksEvery stock in the letter and every new Rex 25 entry gets an evidence grade that day, then is compared with its index (KOSPI or S&P 500) from that day's close to 5, 20 and 60 trading days later. None are left out.
3Rex 25Up to 25 names that clear our quality and trend bar, equally weighted and compared with the index after costs (Rex US Best since 9/11, Rex Korea Best since 9/14).
4The accountIn the US, our live account (time-weighted return vs S&P 500 and QQQ); in Korea, Rex's live account buying monthly (vs KOSPI and KOSDAQ). Percentages only.
Today's stance — hold cash, wait and see, buy in stages or add exposure — is set by four tests: how often our best-graded stocks beat the index after 5 trading days, how the same stocks did after 20, how many names clear the Rex 25 bar, and the market signal light (Rex Compass). The tests and today's values run in every letter.
Whether the grades mean anything was tested on a pre-registered sample of 60 (at five trading days); the result is on the scorecard as it came out. See the full public scorecard → · Our US live account →
