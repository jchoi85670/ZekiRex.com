How we score: the weight of evidence

Each Korean stock in the digest gets four yes/no checks. The number of yeses is the weight of its evidence.

#Check
1Did the price rise?
2Zeki strength 90 or above (a deliberately high bar)
3Net buying by foreign investors
4Net buying by institutions
Results are recorded from the issue-date close to the close 5, 20 and 60 trading days later. Whether this method means anything was tested on a pre-registered sample of 60 (at five trading days), and the result is published on the scorecard as it came out. See the full public scorecard →
