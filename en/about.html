About

Last updated 2026-10-29 03:47 PDT

ZekiRex™ is a research company that reads stock news through published rules and records every day whether those rules were right. Founded on June 20, 2025. Rex is the Founder and CEO.

  • Rules are published first; every change is logged with its reason.
  • Every stock we feature is compared with its index 5, 20 and 60 trading days later. Records are never edited or deleted.
  • Tests use pre-registered criteria and samples, and results are published either way.
  • If the publisher holds a featured stock, that issue says so.
