Wait — the stance the signal implies.

Two things changed while Seoul was shut for three days. Korean lenders were breached by hackers using artificial intelligence, and the G7 agreed to release 100 million barrels of crude and diesel. Both reach prices at once when the market reopens on Tuesday, October 6.

Starting with Shinhan Bank on September 28, about 68,000 personal records leaked from seven financial firms through October 3. Regulators believe a free Chinese open-source AI tool called ARTEX was used. They called bank chiefs to an emergency meeting on the October 4 holiday. (Korea Economic Daily and Maeil Business Newspaper, Oct 5)

The G7 agreed on October 2 local time to release that volume over four months. West Texas Intermediate fell 1.9% to 91.11 dollars a barrel. That came after Seoul's close, so it is not yet in Korean prices. (Maeil Business Newspaper, Oct 5)

In New York on October 2 the Nasdaq Composite rose 1.19% to 27,190.86 after setting an intraday record. The US 10-year Treasury yield rose to 5.27% the same day (Investor's Business Daily, Oct 2).

What to do now
What to do today — three things

Here is what to do now, before Seoul reopens on Tuesday.

① Refiners must first absorb lower oil. S-Oil rose 8.99% on October 2, and that night the G7 release pushed oil down. But jet fuel, which faces no export cap, is selling to Europe at higher prices.

② With the hack spreading across finance, security spending is the question. AhnLab closed at a one-year high on October 2, and foreign investors and institutions both bought over 20 sessions.

③ Seoul reopens on Tuesday, October 6. October 9 is the Hangul Day holiday, so the week has three sessions.

So what do we do: Even with oil lower, Europe is short of jet fuel. The first check after the October 6 open is whether refiners hold their October 2 gains.

Theme 1 — Jet fuel, outside the export cap, heads to Europe

After the US-Iran war the government capped exports of petrol, diesel and kerosene at last year's level. Jet fuel was left out. August jet fuel output was 15.38 million barrels, up 20.1% from a year earlier and the most in seven years.

Jet fuel exports for January to August rose 86.8% to 9.78 billion dollars. In August Europe took 47% of them. European jet fuel margins run 80 to 90 dollars a barrel, about 30 dollars above Asia. (Korea Economic Daily, Oct 5)

Theme 2 — The AI hack that got into Korean finance

The attack went after peripheral systems such as staff work apps rather than internet banking. Some banks' lookup services were wrongly built to show loan applications without identity checks. Woori Bank, which stopped the first attack, runs an AI security system that watches around the clock. (Maeil Business Newspaper, Oct 5)

The attacker switched among 25 internet addresses in more than eight countries. The security industry fears similar attacks will spread to other companies. (Korea Economic Daily, Oct 5)
