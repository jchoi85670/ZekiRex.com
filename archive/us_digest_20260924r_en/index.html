Executive summary — what to do now
Three things to settle before the open.
What to do today
1. The 10-year yield is above 5.1%. Size down any new buys in richly valued growth stocks.
S&P Global's September manufacturing index rose to 57 from 53.9 in August. The services index rose to 58.7 from 56.5. The economy is not cooling, and markets began to price two more Fed rate increases by year-end. A weak Treasury auction and hawkish remarks from a Fed governor added to the move. (WSJ · IBD)
2. On the day yields jumped, security software rose against the tape. That is where to look today.
CrowdStrike rose 5.27%, Palo Alto Networks 5.79% and Okta 7.33%. All three carry a Zeki Strength of 94 or higher. It was the day an AI agent's unauthorised entry to an Australian government site came to light.
3. The AI build-out keeps spending. Trading piled into the distributors that move the hardware.
TD Synnex, which distributes servers and PCs, rose 2.97% on 3.85 times its 50-day volume. Capital spending at five cloud giants is estimated at $4.2 trillion over the four years to 2029. (WSJ)
So what do we do: If you add anything today, make it security software, and keep the size smaller than usual. Small caps can wait until the 10-year is back below 5%.

The Journal's front page said AI spending is becoming the largest economic bet in US history. A Brookings estimate puts data-centre investment at $10.3 trillion over 2025-2032. What it is. A record of the companies we found in the newspapers and named in the digest, marked at that day's close, then counted as the price moves. Grouped by how firm the evidence was.
What it is for. To keep a number on whether names picked from the news actually perform. Each name is recorded on the day it ran, so the winners cannot be chosen afterwards.
What it is not. Not a recommendation, not something we bought, and not yet a verdict. That comes once, from a pre-registered test, when both markets together reach 60 cases.
How long a record. Most of these numbers cover a few days. Prices rise for a few days and fall for a few days; the shorter the window, the more flattering — or the more punishing — it looks.
