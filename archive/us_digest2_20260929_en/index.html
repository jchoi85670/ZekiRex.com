Mideast crude exports are rebounding and Saudi Arabia has resumed shipments through its repaired East-West pipeline. When crude flows again, refiners that turn it into gasoline and diesel gain on input costs. Valero has a Zeki Strength of 95. (WSJ front page)

AMD will buy AI research firm World Labs for about $8.2 billion in stock. Nvidia's board approved another $150 billion of buybacks, lifting its total authorisation to $235 billion. AMD's Zeki Strength of 97 is the highest of today's names. (WSJ front page, Business)
